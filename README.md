# Compilers, Codecs and Language Toolchains: What Agreement Between Builds Can Establish

Aaron Michael Weis · Oneiron Holdings LLC · [ORCID 0009-0002-1906-148X](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-1906-148X) · Research preprint v0.1.0 · 23 November 2026.

**[Read the PDF](compiler-toolchain-survey.pdf)** · [Read as text](PAPER.md) · [Explore the 73-entry catalogue](CATALOGUE.csv) · [Reproduce the experiment](experiment/README.md) · [Version and citation](https://github.com/OneiroNexus/compiler-toolchain-survey/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

Two compilers at two optimization settings agreed on every input, including on a deliberately wrong interpretation of a byte. Checking the answer against the field definition found 128 errors in each signed control. This preprint connects the distinction to a catalogue of 43 compiler and toolchain entries and 30 codec and format entries. It closes with a proposed application to sensor-event replay for the Omega Game Sphere AR engine. COQI is a proposed timing test site; the paper reports no site work, partnership or measured sensor result.

The full catalogue is inside the PDF as well as in the CSV. The numerical record is in [observations.csv](experiment/recorded/observations.csv) and [results.json](experiment/recorded/results.json).
